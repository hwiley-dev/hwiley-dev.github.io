About

I work as an independent systems researcher across AI safety, accessibility, infrastructure, and practical tool design. My focus is turning messy technical questions into concrete research, clear interfaces, and workflows that hold up in real conditions.

That includes AI-assisted research on model behavior and societal risk, accessibility work for blind and low-vision creators, and operational experience in robotics and infrastructure inspection. The through-line is clarity: better feedback, better evaluation, and better outcomes for people using complex systems.
